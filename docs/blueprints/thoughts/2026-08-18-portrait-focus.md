# The whole portrait flies, not just the two machines

> 2026-08-18 · **Status: in force** · Every distinct object on the portrait shares the instrument verb: click flies the camera to a working look. No per-object minigames.

Converged 2026-08-18, after the portrait (research, music, football) was on the desk at the same
rank as the gear, but only the MPC and the mixer could be flown to.

## The principle

**Clicking anything on the portrait flies the camera to it. That is the whole interaction. The
mug does not pour, the football does not bounce, the paper does not open a second product.**

This is the same verb [`2026-08-17-focus-by-flying.md`](2026-08-17-focus-by-flying.md) already
settled for the two instruments — a camera move, not a mode — extended to the rest of the
portrait rather than a new kind of toy. It serves
[`2026-08-15-instrument-over-scene.md`](2026-08-15-instrument-over-scene.md): effort stays on how
the scene is played, and "played" here means looking, not decorating.

## Decided

**The set is every distinct object, not just the two machines.** Today that is the MPC, the
mixer, each monitor, the mug, the preprint, the assembled flute, and the football on the floor.
The desk stays the fly-home click. The books under the monitors are the stand, not a target.

**One rule still serves all of them.** `CameraDirector` already takes a world position and a
distance, and keeps the current viewing angle. `shot.ts` already derives distance from subject
width and occupancy. New targets join that, they do not get a second flight system.

**No per-object minigame.** Raised as the alternative and rejected: a unique verb per prop is the
abandoned 3D room again, just smaller.

## Still open

None. The paper-as-route question closed the same day in
[`2026-08-18-focus-caption.md`](2026-08-18-focus-caption.md): the work may enter; the mug may not.

## What was rejected

**A unique interaction per object** — the framing that opened the elicit. It would spend polish
on the setting, which the governing principle already forbids.
