## Current taxonomy

Use four public-facing groups. Do not nest more groups under them.

1. `Research`
2. `Side Project`
3. `Demo`
4. `Pieces`

`Demo` is **interactive**: the audience operates the work.

`Pieces` is **received**: the audience watches or listens. Directing, composing, producing a show,
and recorded music all belong here.

This split is intentional. Interactive installations stay in `Demo`. Directed musicals, produced
evenings, and later music works stay in `Pieces`. Do not merge them again just because both feel
"creative".

Facets such as `theatre` and `music` are **tags**, not groups. An item belongs to one group
and may carry one or more tags.

Tag vocabulary:

- `music` — MIR, audio, musical interface, or musical theatre
- `theatre` — directed or produced stage work
- `instrument` — physical instrument making
- `health` — healthcare / rehab
- `vision` — visual, AR, or graphics
- `installation` — physical interactive installation
- `package` — open-source / pip-installable tooling
